How to count calories and macros

Enter the amount eaten and the nutrition-label values per 100 g. Start with an example food, then adapt its figures to the actual product.

Worked calculation

If 100 g of oats provides 379 kcal, then 150 g provides 379 × 1.5 = 568.5 kcal. The same proportion is applied to protein, carbohydrate and fat.

Understanding the totals

Food labels and databases are estimates. Preparation, brand and serving size affect the result. This counter helps reveal trends; it does not diagnose or prescribe a diet.

Data and backups

Edit, duplicate or remove foods and download every record as CSV or JSON. All language versions read the same local record.

Privacy: foods are stored only in this browser in their own space. They remain separate from the journal and main app and are not sent to a server.
