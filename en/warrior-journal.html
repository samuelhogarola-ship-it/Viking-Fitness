How to keep a workout journal

Record enough to compare sessions: exercise, sets, repetitions and load. Add meals and observations when they help explain energy, performance or recovery.

Training volume

Volume is sets × reps × kg for each exercise, added across the day. It is a useful reference but does not replace intensity, technique or exercise selection.

Backups

Download one JSON file with your whole history. Import preview shows the number and date range before you choose merge or replace; the final action always asks for confirmation.

Independent tools

This journal does not receive results automatically. The calorie counter and main app keep separate data.

Privacy: the journal is stored only in this browser. A downloaded backup is under your control, so keep it secure.
