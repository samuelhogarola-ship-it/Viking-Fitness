How to play Viking Sudoku

Fill every row, column and 3 × 3 box with the numbers 1 to 9 without repeats. Choose a cell and use your keyboard or the number pad.

Difficulty and help

There are 20 easy, 20 medium and 20 hard boards. A hint fills one valid cell; three mistakes end the current attempt. Check reports how much of the board is filled.

Private progress and offline play

The board, timer and records are stored locally and shared across languages. Email sync is optional. After the first visit, the app can continue to work offline.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Does changing language reset my game? No: every page uses the same progress keys.
