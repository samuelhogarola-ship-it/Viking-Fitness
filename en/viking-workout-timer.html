How to use the workout timer

Simple rest

A single countdown for breaks between sets. The finish signal means you do not need to watch the screen.

Intervals

Set preparation, work, rest and rounds. The horn marks each work start and a separate sound closes the session.

EMOM

Each round begins when the selected time elapses. Finish your reps, then rest for the remaining seconds.

Sound and safety

Start at low volume, especially with headphones. Audio is generated locally; no recording is downloaded. If vibration or screen wake lock is unavailable, the clock continues with written announcements.

Privacy and offline use: only mode, sound, volume, vibration and screen preferences are stored on this device. Workout history is not stored.
