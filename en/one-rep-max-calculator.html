How the 1RM calculator works

A one-rep max is the estimated heaviest load you could lift once with sound technique. This tool uses the Epley formula: weight × (1 + repetitions ÷ 30).

Worked example

Five repetitions with 100 kg produce 100 × (1 + 5 ÷ 30) = 116.7 kg. The tool rounds that to a practical 116.5 kg.

Reading the percentages

Lower loads favour technique and speed, middle loads help accumulate volume, and higher loads lean towards strength. They are general references: exercise, experience, fatigue and programming all change the response.

Limits and safety

Accuracy falls as repetitions rise, so the calculator accepts no more than 12. Do not test a true maximum without suitable technique, preparation, safety equipment and supervision where needed.

Privacy: exercise, weight, repetitions and results are neither saved nor sent off the device.
