How BMR is calculated

We use the Mifflin–St Jeor equations for adults: male = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5; female = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161.

Worked example

A 30-year-old man weighing 80 kg at 180 cm has an estimated BMR of 1,780 kcal/day. With moderate activity, estimated maintenance is 2,759 kcal/day.

Important limitations

This is a population estimate, not a laboratory measurement. It is not designed for under-18s, pregnancy, breastfeeding or clinical situations. Actual needs vary with body composition, activity and individual response.
