Practical tools for purposeful training

You do not need an account to estimate a load, start an interval or play Sudoku. Tools with a history save data only in this browser and let you delete it at any time.

Where should I begin?

Use the 1RM calculator to orient training loads, the timer during a session and the journal to keep a record. Metabolism and calorie estimates are educational starting points, not medical diagnoses or prescriptions.
